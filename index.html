    • Mapping 1: Free fall → Thunder

    Use case: a drop alarm for a phone. When the phone falls freely for more than about 30 cm (≈0.2 s of near-zero acceleration), a thunder sound is played. Thunder is sudden, loud and low-pitched, which matches the sudden and uncontrolled nature of a fall and immediately draws the owner's attention, like "something bad just happened". The sound is triggered only once per fall, so it is a clear alarm rather than continuous noise.
